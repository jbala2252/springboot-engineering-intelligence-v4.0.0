# Engineering Knowledge
Capture decisions with context, decision, alternatives, consequences, status and validation.
Record patterns only after real use and tests. Link knowledge to source, modules and versions.
Mark stale knowledge for review. Never invent owners, approvals, dates or standards; never store secrets
or sensitive production data.
