# Production Readiness

Review configuration/secrets, resource requests/limits, probes, graceful shutdown, autoscaling,
timeouts, retries, circuit breakers, connection pools, scheduler behavior, logging, metrics,
tracing, alerting, backup/recovery, deployment strategy, rollback, and runbooks. Adapt to actual
deployment artifacts and platform. Identify operational failure scenarios and actionable gaps.
Do not add platform assumptions or dependencies without evidence.
